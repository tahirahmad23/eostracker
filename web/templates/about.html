NAME

eos_tracker - network infrastructure lifecycle management utility

SYNOPSIS

eos_tracker [--monitor] [--alert] devices...

DESCRIPTION

EOS Tracker is a specialized utility designed for network engineers to track End-of-Support (EOS) and End-of-Life (EOL) dates for critical infrastructure hardware.

Unlike generic inventory systems, EOS Tracker focuses specifically on the vendor support lifecycle, providing automated alerts at T-minus 365, 180, 90, and 30 days before support termination.
